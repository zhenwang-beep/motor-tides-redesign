In the middle of it.

3657 Motor Avenue sits in Palms, on the Culver City border. Motor Avenue runs at an angle to the grid around it, which is why so much is close in so many directions.

Getting around

The nearest E Line stop is Palms.

Four tenths of a mile up Motor Avenue — closer than Culver City station, which most listings for this stretch name instead. From Palms the E Line runs west to Santa Monica and east to Downtown LA. The 10 and the 405 are both a short drive, and the Ballona Creek Trail carries on west to the ocean.
