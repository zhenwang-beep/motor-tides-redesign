Motor MidwayBy Wiseman

Three redesign directions

One property, one house style, three ideas.

Three complete seven-page websites for Motor Midway by Wiseman at 3657 Motor Ave., Los Angeles, CA 90034 — each built on the same Wiseman system that runs Motor Tides, so the two properties read as one operator. They carry the property’s real content: the four current floor plans and their rents, every published amenity, the pet policy, all 47 photographs, the seven Matterport tours and the live application portal. They differ in exactly one thing — the idea that carries them.

Direction 01

Golden Hour

The property edited as a single evening, cream paper warming into dusk.

Ten frames of the rooftop at golden hour are the best thing this property owns, so the page is sequenced by the clock — daylight interiors, afternoon patios, the roof at sunset, the building lit after dark — and the palette travels with it. A fixed hour rail marks where in the evening you are reading.

Direction 02

The Crossing

A drawing set. Motor Avenue's diagonal as the organising line.

Motor Avenue runs at an angle to every street it meets, and 3657 is the building at the crossing. This direction treats the site like an architect's drawing set — a drawn street plan, mono annotations carrying the measured facts, and the building read floor by floor from the roof down to the garage.

Direction 03

Open Door

Built on the threshold — every residence here opens onto its own patio.

Patio/Balcony is on every residence, not a subset — in a 692-square-foot two-bedroom that patio is the extra room. This direction is built on the threshold: paired photographs of the room and what the room opens onto, a hairline between them that widens as you scroll. Domestic and human-scale rather than monumental.

How these were built

Inherited, not invented

All three use the Motor Tides design system — the cream paper, the teal ink, Newsreader over Montserrat, the squared buttons and letterspaced labels. The single value that changes is the accent, from Tides’ cool coastal tone to a burnt ochre that agrees with Midway’s golden-hour photography. It is measured at 4.76:1 on cream, so it passes AA as body text.

Content is the live site’s

  • Four plans, real rents and real availability
  • Every community and apartment amenity, verbatim
  • Pet policy, weight limit and breed restrictions, verbatim
  • Utilities note carried on every plan page
  • SecureCAFE apply and resident portals, live

Photography

No image is re-hosted. Every photograph is served from the property’s own RentCafe library — the same origin the live site uses — with Cloudinary generating a real srcset. The whole deployed bundle is a few hundred kilobytes of HTML and CSS.

Two things to confirm

The live homepage gives the leasing number as (760) 212-6707, while the contact page and the footer both give +1 310-853-1532. These concepts use the 310 number throughout.

The photo gallery captions name three plans — Madison, Overland and Palm — that are not on the availability page.

These are design concepts. The contact forms are not wired to a mailbox, and the map is an OpenStreetMap embed rather than the property’s mapping vendor. Everything else — plans, rents, amenities, photography, tours, the apply and resident portals — points at the real thing.
