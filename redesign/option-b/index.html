Brand new on the Motor Avenue corridor

Motor Tides is a brand new residential community now open at 3557 Motor Ave. Choose from thoughtfully designed one, two, three, and four bedroom residences, each finished for elevated everyday living.

Positioned along the vibrant Motor Avenue corridor, Motor Tides puts you minutes from Culver City's acclaimed restaurant row, the Helms Bakery District, independent cafés, boutique retail, and the Ballona Creek Trail — with Sony Pictures, Amazon MGM, and Apple TV+'s Culver City campus all nearby.

With over forty-five years of experience, Wiseman Residential designs its properties with Los Angeles' savvy renter in mind.
