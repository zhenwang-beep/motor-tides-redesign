[01] The Property

Thoughtfully designed for elevated everyday living.

Motor Tides is a brand new residential community now open at 3557 Motor Ave in the heart of Culver City’s most walkable neighborhood. Choose from thoughtfully designed one, two, three, and four bedroom residences, each finished for elevated everyday living.

Positioned along the vibrant Motor Avenue corridor, Motor Tides puts you minutes from Culver City’s acclaimed restaurant row, the Helms Bakery District, independent cafés, boutique retail, and the Ballona Creek Trail — with the Expo/Culver E Line and fast access to Santa Monica, Marina del Rey, and Downtown LA close at hand.

In-person and virtual tours available by appointment, seven days a week.
