Wide shot

Open layouts, blocked for the evening light.

Living, dining, and kitchen in one continuous frame. Expansive windows run the width of the room, so the blue hour arrives everywhere at once — and large private balconies or patios in many homes keep the scene going outside.

Quartz counters. Stainless steel. Late dinners.
