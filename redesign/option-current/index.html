MOTOR TIDES BY WISEMAN

Motor Tides is a brand new residential community now open at 3557 Motor Ave in the heart of Culver City's most walkable neighborhood. Choose from thoughtfully designed one, two, three, and four bedroom residences, each finished for elevated everyday living.

Positioned along the vibrant Motor Avenue corridor, Motor Tides puts you minutes from Culver City's acclaimed restaurant row, the Helms Bakery District, independent cafés, boutique retail, and the Ballona Creek Trail. Major entertainment studios, Sony Pictures, Amazon MGM, and Apple TV+'s Culver City campus, are nearby, as are the Expo/Culver E Line station and fast access to Santa Monica, Marina del Rey, and Downtown LA.

At Motor Tides, you'll enjoy a rooftop deck with sweeping city views as well as a fitness center. As a pet-friendly building, your cats and dogs are also welcome.

In-person and virtual tours available by appointment. Contact our leasing team at (310) 564-6408.
