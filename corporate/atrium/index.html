What Wiseman does

One company, from the drawing to the door.

The industry splits the work; Wiseman does all of it, and keeps what it builds. The person who answers about a repair works for the company that poured the foundation.

01

Develop

New apartments where the city needs housing — most recently the Motor Avenue corridor in Palms.

02

Build

Built in-house, through the family’s licensed California construction arm.

03

Own

Held, not flipped — seventy-two buildings on our own books.

04

Manage, on site

Each building keeps its own office, leasing line and maintenance team.

The on-site model

Repairs and questions go to the building’s own office — not a call centre in another city.

The name on the door is a person you can find — the one thing residents tell us they value most.

Our History

The record, as it stands.

    Four records, four founding years [CLIENT]

    LinkedIn says 1980. Contractor licence 417744 carries an issue date of February 1982. Wiseman’s own Archinect profile says 1985. The Better Business Bureau has June 1987. No year is printed here until Wiseman confirms one.

    LinkedIn · CSLB · Archinect · BBB

  2. “My father built apartment buildings throughout my childhood.”

    A Westside builder, in his own words

    The Los Angeles Business Journal profiled Isaac Cohanzad, then president of Wiseman Development Co., with 63 homes rising on Santa Monica Boulevard and 34 in Glendale.

    Los Angeles Business Journal, 29 August 2010

  3. One claim held for eleven years

    Wiseman put its portfolio online in 2013. Through two rebuilds of the site one sentence survived verbatim — that its three- and four-bedroom plans are rare. The feed still bears it out: 17 buildings offer four bedrooms, 5 go to five.

    Internet Archive captures 2014–2025 · live leasing feed

  4. Ground-up, and mostly in Palms

    Filings on Lincoln Boulevard, then East Venice. Motor Avenue broke ground in 2021 and again in 2022. Over the same stretch the trade press put the portfolio at around thirty buildings, then nearly seventy.

    Urbanize LA · The Real Deal, 2022 and 2024

  5. Seven storeys on Motor Avenue

    Motor Tides finished in 2026 — 104 apartments with three accessory dwellings, 107 homes in all. In March, Wiseman filed for 490 more on Venice Boulevard.

    Urbanize LA, May and March 2026
