The building’s own description

Motor Tides is a brand-new seven-storey building at 3557 Motor Avenue, on the Motor Avenue corridor minutes from Culver City. One, two, three and four-bedroom residences, a rooftop deck with city views, a fitness centre and a pet-friendly policy. In-person and virtual tours by appointment.
