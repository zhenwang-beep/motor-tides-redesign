Beverly Grove

14 buildings Third Street · Beverly Center · Miracle Mile Rent $1,700–$5,995

Fourteen buildings inside a half-mile of the busiest corner of the Westside.

Third Street's restaurant row, the Beverly Center and Cedars-Sinai are all within walking distance. The Grove and the Original Farmers Market sit at Third and Fairfax. The streets themselves — Croft, Orlando, Kings, Sweetzer, Hayworth — stay residential and quiet one block off the traffic.
