Brentwood

13 buildings San Vicente · Barrington · Montana Rent $1,805–$5,400

Low buildings under old trees, north of Wilshire and south of Sunset.

San Vicente Boulevard runs through the middle of it, the coral trees down its median designated a Los Angeles Historic‑Cultural Monument and the grass beside them used as the neighbourhood’s running track. The Brentwood Country Mart, the Sunday farmers market and the Getty Center are all within a short drive.
