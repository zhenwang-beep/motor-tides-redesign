West Los Angeles

27 buildings Sawtelle · Santa Monica Blvd · Westgate Rent $2,495–$6,595

The densest stretch of the portfolio. Wiseman owns on nearly every north–south street between Santa Monica Boulevard and Olympic.

Sawtelle Japantown runs along Sawtelle Boulevard with its ramen counters, izakayas and nurseries. The Expo Line stops at Bundy and at Sepulveda; the 405 and the 10 meet a few blocks south. UCLA and Westwood Village are ten minutes north, Santa Monica ten minutes west.
