California

Opt out, and limit sensitive data.

  • Your choices

    Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information.

  • Does Wiseman sell or share

    Wiseman does not sell personal information for money. Whether any disclosure counts as a “sale” or a “share” as the CPRA defines those terms is being confirmed with counsel. [CLIENT]

  • How to opt out

    The opt-out route — a form, an email address or a toll-free number — and whether the site honours Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals are confirmed before launch. [CLIENT]

  • Authorised agents

    You may use an authorised agent to make a request on your behalf. We may ask the agent for proof and ask you to verify your own identity.

  • No discrimination

    We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

  • How we verify a request

    To protect your information we confirm your identity before acting on a request. The exact steps are set with counsel. [CLIENT]
