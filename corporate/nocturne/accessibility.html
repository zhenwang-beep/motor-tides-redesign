Accessibility.

How we build this site to be usable by everyone, and how to reach a person if something falls short.

This is a draft for the client’s counsel to confirm before launch. [CLIENT]

Accessibility statement

Built in, not bolted on.

  • Our commitment

    Wiseman Residential aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and to keep the site usable with a keyboard, a screen reader, or the browser zoomed in.

  • What we have done

    Skip link and focus. A skip link to the main content, and a visible focus outline on every interactive element.

    Keyboard parity. Anything that works on hover also works on focus.

    Usable without the map. The building list stays complete and usable with the map switched off.

    Alt text. On the brand marks and on the Equal Housing Opportunity mark.

    Reduced motion. Animation collapses to a still end state when the browser asks for it.

  • No accessibility overlay

    This site does not use a third-party accessibility overlay or widget. Those tools can interfere with assistive technology; we prefer to build access into the site itself.

  • Third-party and ongoing content

    The resident and applicant portal is a separate system with its own accessibility. Photography is hotlinked from the leasing feed. If something on the site falls short, tell us and we will fix it.

  • Conformance

    The site is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 AA: most of it meets the standard and we are working toward full conformance. It has not yet had a formal third-party audit. [CLIENT]

    Partially conformant

  • How to reach us

    Call +1 310-473-3000 or write to the corporate office at 1520 Federal Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90025. A dedicated accessibility contact and a response-time commitment are confirmed before launch. [CLIENT]

  • Reasonable accommodation

    To request a reasonable accommodation, contact the building's own office or the corporate office. How Wiseman handles those requests is the company's to set out. [CLIENT]
