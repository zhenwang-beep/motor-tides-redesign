The seven areas

  1. Area 01 — Sawtelle · Santa Monica Blvd · Westgate

    West Los Angeles

    The densest stretch of the portfolio. Wiseman owns on nearly every north–south street between Santa Monica Boulevard and Olympic.

    Sawtelle Japantown runs along Sawtelle Boulevard with its ramen counters, izakayas and nurseries. The Expo Line stops at Bundy and at Sepulveda; the 405 and the 10 meet a few blocks south. UCLA and Westwood Village are ten minutes north, Santa Monica ten minutes west.

  2. Area 03 — Third Street · Beverly Center · Miracle Mile

    Beverly Grove

    Fourteen buildings inside a half-mile of the busiest corner of the Westside.

    Third Street's restaurant row, the Beverly Center and Cedars-Sinai are all within walking distance. The Grove and the Original Farmers Market sit at Third and Fairfax. The streets themselves — Croft, Orlando, Kings, Sweetzer, Hayworth — stay residential and quiet one block off the traffic.

  3. Area 02 — San Vicente · Barrington · Montana

    Brentwood

    Low buildings under old trees, north of Wilshire and south of Sunset.

    San Vicente Boulevard runs through the middle of it, the coral trees down its median designated a Los Angeles Historic-Cultural Monument and the grass beside them used as the neighborhood's running track. The Brentwood Country Mart, the Sunday farmers market and the Getty Center are all within a short drive.

  4. Area 04 — Melrose · Fairfax · Larchmont

    Hollywood

    The working side of Hollywood — studio blocks, bungalow courts and side streets off Melrose.

    Paramount Pictures is on Melrose, Sunset Bronson and Netflix are on Sunset, and Runyon Canyon is at the top of the hill. The Hollywood Farmers Market takes over Ivar on Sundays; Larchmont Village keeps its own main street a few blocks east.

  5. Area 05 — Abbot Kinney · Penmar · Rose

    Venice

    Two buildings, a mile from the water.

    Abbot Kinney Boulevard and Rose Avenue are the neighborhood's two commercial spines; the canals, the Penmar golf course and the beach path are all within a short ride.

  6. Area 06 — Motor Avenue · minutes from Culver City

    Palms · Motor Avenue

    The newest ground Wiseman has built on, and the address of the flagship.

    Motor Avenue runs north from downtown Culver City through Palms. Sony Pictures, Amazon MGM and Apple TV+ all have campuses within a few minutes, the Helms Bakery District is close, and the Expo/Culver City E Line station puts Santa Monica at one end of the train and Downtown at the other. The address is in the City of Los Angeles; Culver City begins a few blocks south.
