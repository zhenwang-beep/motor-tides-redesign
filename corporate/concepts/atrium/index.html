What Wiseman does

One company, from the drawing to the door.

Most of the industry splits the work — a developer builds, a fund buys, a third-party operator manages. Wiseman does all of it, and keeps what it builds. That is why the person who answers about a repair works for the same company that poured the foundation.

01

Develop

We design and entitle new apartments where Los Angeles needs housing — most recently the Motor Avenue corridor in Palms.

02

Build

We build them ourselves, through the family's licensed California construction arm — not a rotating cast of contractors.

03

Own

We hold what we build. Seventy-two buildings on our own books, not a portfolio assembled to be flipped.

04

Manage, on site

Every building keeps its own office, its own leasing line and its own maintenance team — named people, in the building, on the street.

The on-site model

Repairs and questions go to the building's own office — not a call centre in another city.

The name on the door is a person you can find. It is the one thing residents tell us they value most, so we built the whole company around it.
