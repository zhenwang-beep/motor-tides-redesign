Brentwood

San Vicente · Barrington · Montana

About Brentwood

Low buildings under old trees, north of Wilshire and south of Sunset.

San Vicente Boulevard runs through the middle of it, the coral trees down its median designated a Los Angeles Historic-Cultural Monument and the grass beside them used as the neighborhood's running track. The Brentwood Country Mart, the Sunday farmers market and the Getty Center are all within a short drive.
