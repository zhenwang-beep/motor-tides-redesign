What each of them fixes

The current site puts two nav items and a photo slider in front of a Yardi search page. It buries the thing Wiseman actually has that nobody else does — a specific set of Los Angeles streets, twenty-seven buildings deep in West LA alone, named after the streets they stand on.

All four directions share the same spine: a marketplace-grade search with filters and a real map, a building page for every address with today's floor plans and availability, and the same checked facts throughout.

Every page is real. Seventy-two building pages, seven neighbourhood pages, search, residents, company and contact — 86 pages per direction, generated from the live feed, with every link resolving and every photograph loading from Wiseman's own library.

An earlier direction, Five Bedrooms, organised the portfolio by home size; it is retired but still browsable at fivebed/.

Two things to check with Wiseman first

Research turned up two claims on the current homepage that public sources do not support. None of the four sites repeats them.

01“Over 45 years.” The company's own archived homepage said “over 25 years” as recently as 7 September 2025, and their Archinect profile says the firm was established in 1985. The sites lead with checkable facts instead.
02“Over 100+ apartment communities.” Their own leasing feed returns 72. Every count on these sites is computed from that feed, so it is right today and corrects itself tomorrow.

One geographic correction is already applied throughout: Motor Tides at 3557 Motor Avenue is in the City of Los Angeles — Palms — a few blocks from the Culver City line.
